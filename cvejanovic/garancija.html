GarancijaWarranty

Doživotna garancija na implante i petogodišnja na krunice, fasete i mostove.A lifetime warranty on implants and five years on crowns, veneers and bridges.

Implanti: doživotna garancijaImplants: lifetime warranty

  • Izgubljen implant na kome nema nadoknade: ordinacija ga zamenjuje o svom trošku, ako kost to dozvoljava.Implant lost with no prosthesis attached: the clinic replaces it at its own cost, if the bone allows.
  • Izgubljen implant sa nadoknadom, uz redovne kontrole: zamenjuju se i implant i nadoknada.Implant lost with a prosthesis, with regular check-ups: both the implant and the prosthesis are replaced.
  • Ako novi implant nije moguć, radi se augmentacija kosti ili se menja protetski plan.If a new implant is not possible, bone augmentation is done or the prosthetic plan is changed.

Krunice, fasete, mostovi: 5 godinaCrowns, veneers, bridges: 5 years

  • Besplatna zamena nakon većeg okrnjenja keramike ili loma konstrukcije mosta.Free replacement after major ceramic chipping or a fractured bridge structure.
  • Promena boje ili oblika posle probe nije pokrivena garancijom.Changes of colour or shape after the try-in are not covered.
Uslovi:Conditions: kontrola na svakih šest meseci i panoramski snimak jednom godišnje. Garancija ne pokriva fizička oštećenja, kao ni troškove prevoza, smeštaja i druge lične troškove.check-ups every six months and a panoramic X-ray once a year. The warranty does not cover physical damage or transport, accommodation and other private costs.
